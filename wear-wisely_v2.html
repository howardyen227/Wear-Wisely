How scores work.

Four weighted categories

Environment (35%) — climate targets, materials, chemicals, waste. Labour (30%) — living wages, supply-chain audits, worker rights. Transparency (20%) — supplier disclosure and reporting, informed by the Fashion Transparency Index. Animal welfare (15%) — use and sourcing of animal-derived materials.


Sources

Each brand is evaluated using credible public sources specific to that brand — investigative journalism (BBC, Reuters, The Guardian), NGO and watchdog reports (Clean Clothes Campaign, Human Rights Watch, Stand.earth, Changing Markets, Public Eye, Remake, Fashion Revolution), certification bodies (B Lab, SBTi) and the brand's own published reports. Every finding is linked to its source and tagged by category.


Important

Scores are an editorial summary for quick guidance, not an official rating. Public ratings change over time — always follow the source links for the most current information.
